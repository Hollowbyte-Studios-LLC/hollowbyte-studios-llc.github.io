_Last updated: April 18, 2026_

**HollowByte Studio LLC** (“we”, “our”, “us”) built the **WordFlurry** mobile game as an entertainment application.  
This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the WordFlurry app (the “App”).

---

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect certain information automatically and through third-party services to improve the App and provide a better user experience.

### a. Analytics Data

We use **Google Cloud Console** for analytics to collect non-personal data such as:

- App usage and performance metrics
- Device information (model, OS version, language)
- Session duration and engagement data

This helps us understand how players interact with the game.  
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

### b. Advertising Data

We use **Google AdMob** to serve ads. AdMob may collect data such as:

- Device identifiers (e.g., Android Advertising ID)
- IP address (for approximate location and ad relevance)
- Interaction with advertisements

AdMob may use this information to personalize ads unless you disable ad personalization in your device settings.  
[AdMob Privacy Information](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543)

### c. Google Play Games Services

If you sign in with **Google Play Games**, Google may collect information related to your player profile and achievements.  
This data is managed by Google and subject to Google’s Privacy Policy.

### d. In-App Purchases

We offer **in-app purchases** to allow users to remove ads from the game.  
All transactions are handled securely through Google Play.

---

## 2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information to:

- Improve gameplay, performance, and user experience
- Analyze app performance and fix bugs
- Display relevant ads
- Support leaderboards, achievements, and cloud saves

We **do not sell or rent** user data to third parties.

---

## 3. Data Retention

We retain analytics and advertising data only as long as necessary for the purposes described above or as required by Google’s data policies.

---

## 4. Children’s Privacy

WordFlurry is designed for general audiences.  
We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the EU).  
If we learn that a child has provided personal data, we will delete it promptly.

---

## 5. Your Choices

- **Opt-out of Ad Personalization:** Turn off ad personalization in your Google Account or device settings.
- **Analytics Opt-out:** Disable analytics through your device’s privacy settings or limit data sharing by adjusting app permissions.
- **Google Play Games:** You can sign out of Play Games at any time through your device settings.

---

## 6. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical measures to protect your data.  
However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.
